# Prerequisites 

here are some of the key aspects to think of before starting your engineering project 

1. preparing the site
 - make sure you clear all weeds and find a source for your water needs
 - soil testing - may not be necessary for all sites but is highly advised 
2. have an engineer draw and submit the plans to necessary authorities to get the building permit
 - note that the building permit is mostly valid for 3 - 5 years 
3. make sure you have the plan  laid out for winter months so that the construction is not obscured by weather events
	- rain and water source availability
	- move your construction to summer months and complete roofing before the rain shows up
